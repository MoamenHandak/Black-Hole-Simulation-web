Physics & Relativistic Raytracing

This application simulates general relativistic light deflection around a Schwarzschild black hole in real-time WebGL2.

  • Event Horizon ($R_s = 2GM/c^2$): The surface boundary where escape velocity equals the speed of light.
  • Gravitational Lensing: Curvature of spacetime deflects light rays around the central mass.
  • Photon Sphere ($1.5 R_s$): Unstable circular orbit for light, forming the bright ring around the shadow ($R_{shadow} \approx 2.65 R_s$).
  • Doppler Beaming: Material approaching the viewer is blueshifted and amplified; material receding is dimmed and redshifted.
